About the Area Converter

Area units span an enormous range — from a square millimeter on a circuit board to a square mile of farmland — and the metric, imperial, and land-survey systems rarely line up cleanly. A property listing in acres, a floor plan in square meters, a country's size in square kilometers: this tool lets you enter a value once and see it converted to every common area unit at the same time.

How to Use This Tool

  1. Enter the area value you want to convert.
  2. Select the unit that value is currently in.
  3. The table updates automatically, showing the equivalent value in every other unit.

Conversion Formula

Every area unit converts through the square meter (m²). To convert from unit A to unit B:

value_B = value_A × factor_A→m² ÷ factor_B→m²

Area factors are the square of the corresponding length factors — for example, since 1 ft = 0.3048 m exactly, 1 ft² = 0.3048² = 0.09290304 m² exactly. All conversions here are pure multiplication or division.

Worked Examples

Example 1 — Square Meters to Square Feet

A 150 m² apartment: 150 ÷ 0.09290304 m²/ft² ≈ 1,614.6 ft².

Example 2 — Acres to Hectares

A 40-acre farm: 40 × 4,046.8564224 m²/ac ÷ 10,000 m²/ha ≈ 16.19 hectares.

Example 3 — Square Kilometers to Square Miles

A city covering 500 km²: 500 × 1,000,000 m² ÷ 2,589,988.110336 m²/mi² ≈ 193.05 mi².

Example 4 — Square Inches to Square Centimeters

A 20 in² sticker: 20 × 6.4516 cm²/in² = 129.032 cm².

Frequently Asked Questions

How many square feet are in a square meter?
1 m² ≈ 10.7639 ft². Conversely, 1 ft² = 0.09290304 m² exactly.
How many square meters are in an acre?
1 acre = 4,046.8564224 m², or about 4,047 m². It's easiest to remember as roughly 0.4 hectares, or about 40% of a soccer pitch.
What's the difference between an acre and a hectare?
A hectare is exactly 10,000 m² (a 100 m × 100 m square). An acre is 4,046.8564224 m² — about 2.47 acres make up one hectare.
How do I convert square kilometers to square miles?
Divide by 2.589988110336. Equivalently, 1 mi² ≈ 2.59 km², so 1 km² ≈ 0.386 mi².
Why isn't 1 ft² simply 12× smaller than 1 yd²?
Because area scales with the square of length, not linearly. Since 1 yard = 3 feet, 1 yd² = 3² = 9 ft² — not 3 ft². This is the same reason area units in general don't convert with the same factor as their corresponding length units.
